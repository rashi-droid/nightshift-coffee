(05)Why Nightshift

Made bypeople whodon't sleep.

We started brewing in a borrowed kitchen between midnight and six, because that's when the kitchen was free. The hours stuck. So did the coffee.

01

Small batch,
every night.

Every batch is steeped overnight in 200-litre tanks and canned the next morning. Small enough to taste, big enough to keep up with you.

02

Real ingredients.
Short labels.

Coffee, water, and the thing on the front of the can. Real vanilla, real cocoa, real roasted hazelnut. If we can't pronounce it, it doesn't go in.

03

Roasted for
the cold.

Hot-brew roasts go flat when chilled. Ours are profiled specifically for cold extraction, so you get chocolate and fruit instead of cardboard.
